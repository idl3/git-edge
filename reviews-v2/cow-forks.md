# Second-pass review: Copy-on-write forks

# Review v2: cow-forks (idea #22)

## Scores
- Feasibility 4/5 (was 4). Every host call exists in `worker` 0.8.5 source: `env.durable_object("REPO").id_from_name(..).get_stub()`, `Stub::fetch_with_request`, `Request::new_with_init` + `RequestInit::{with_method, with_body}` (source-verified in the two-phase-push review, runtime-unverified), sync `SqlStorage::exec`, `json_each`, `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT` (SQLite >= 3.24). Unverified with fallbacks shown: `worker::Bucket: Clone` for `for_repo` (line 207; fallback `env.bucket("BUCKET")`), `SqlStorageValue: From<Option<String>>` for `peeled` (line 143). It does not compile as written: `PinPack`/`ObjRec` derive only `Deserialize` yet are serialized into the begin/page responses (lines 99-100, 112), and `Vec<RefRow>` serializes only if `ObjectId`/`BString` get `serde`, which the pinned manifest does not enable (memo section 4). Mechanical, but real.
- Reliability 3/5 (was 2). The first-pass data-loss path is closed by construction: pin insert and the `NOT IN pack_pins` dead-mark are sync spans in the same DO and serialize (platform-facts #4), and every import step replays idempotently. Residuals: a `Fork` job that goes `dead` (parent unreachable for the ~2 h retry window of 4.4) leaves `fork_state` stuck mid-import with no route that re-enqueues it; one "unit of work" is a page of up to 20,000 sequential `INSERT ... WHERE EXISTS` statements with no `spent_80pct` check (A4); `fork_detach` accepts a mid-import fork (line 70).
- Correctness 3/5 (was 3). The snapshot design is right — pins over the whole live set inside `begin`'s span, `page` joined to `pack_pins` so the fork can never reference unpinned bytes. But the owner redirect is registered for only two of the five paths that read pack bytes, so a fork's own `fetch`, `GcMark`/`GcConsolidate` and detach all fail with `Storage` as written, and `INSERT OR IGNORE` on `refs` means `fork_sync` never moves a ref. The mechanism is correct; the registry of edits is not.
- Effort: weeks. The module itself is days; the real cost is the six listed foundation edits plus the three it missed, all inside sync spans and read paths that already exist.

## Contract compliance
Matches: REGISTRY block per A9 (3 edge routes, 6 DO routes, `JobKind::Fork`, tables `forks`/`pack_pins`/`borrowed`/`fork_unpins`, meta keys `parent`/`fork_state`, no new R2 prefixes per 2.2); every new DO route is a "none"-awaits sync span per the 1.3 table; the fork claim is `ON CONFLICT` + `changes()` inside one span (3, platform-facts #1); the page cursor is the `objects` primary key `(sha, pack_id)` so same-sha duplicates across packs cannot straddle a page (2.3); `json_each(?)` for the unpin list under the 100-bound-parameter cap (A6); `enqueue`-only with `rearm` after the span, never `set_alarm` (4.1, A3); `owner_repo` is `meta.repo_id`, immutable under rename (8.2, 8.4); exactly two added scenarios, the stated maximum (11).
Deviations (each also in the structured list):
1. The owner redirect is listed for `pack::generate::write_pack` and the `push/lookup` ingest path only (lines 204-205). Three more paths read pack bytes under `r/<self.repo>/packs/<id>` and will get `Storage("missing ...")` on any fork: `send_set`'s commit-region reads and tree rounds (9.3, 9.5), `GcMark`'s section-9 round loop (5.1, "the same loop shape"), and `GcConsolidate`'s verbatim `read_entries` copy of candidate packs (5.2) — which is exactly the copy detach depends on. Both added scenarios fail as written.
2. `INSERT OR IGNORE INTO refs` (line 142) makes `/_do/fork/sync` a no-op for moved or deleted upstream refs; Known limits promises a post-begin push "is invisible until `/_do/fork/sync`" (line 237) — the objects arrive but the ref stays at the snapshot. Mechanical: upsert plus delete-not-in-set.
3. `run_slice` never calls `SliceBudget::spent_80pct()` (A4), and a single unit is up to 20,000 per-row inserts (lines 153-158). A page that cannot finish inside the 20 s slice wall clock (4.3) is retried whole every firing until the job goes `dead` after 8 attempts (4.4) — deterministic livelock for a slow parent page, leaving the fork in `importing` and the parent's pins held forever.
4. Serialize drift, mechanical: `PinPack` (line 40) and `ObjRec` (line 42) are `Deserialize`-only but are serialized; `RefRow` (1.1) has no `Serialize` derive and `ObjectId`/`BString` have none under the pinned features.
5. Cross-proof drift: `/_do/push/lookup` returns `locs: Vec<Option<ObjLoc>>` in two-phase-push (its line 93); this proof needs `{loc, owner}` per id (line 205). `meta_opt` (line 206) and `d.env()` (line 184) are not among the sibling's helpers (`meta`, `meta_str`, `meta_i64`, `q`, `changes` exist; `meta_opt` does not). `list_refs` in refs-sqlite-objects-r2 sets `peeled: None` unconditionally (its line 62), so the A5 `peeled` column never reaches `begin`'s response and fork clones lose `peeled:` lines.
6. `fork_detach` has no `fork_state` gate (lines 70-74): detaching during `pinning`/`importing` flips `released` mid-import; the drain arm then finishes an index mixing pinned, unpinned and consolidated rows. Survivable once deviation 1 is fixed (marked objects are consolidated out first), but unanalysed.
7. The header still carries the template placeholders `__V2_VERDICT__`, `__F__/5` (line 3). Mechanical.

## First-pass blockers
1. "`gc-and-repack-alarm` ... sweeps unmarked loose objects, and explicitly plans to delete loose objects once a pack covers them ... every inherited object the fork points at is gone from R2": **resolved**. Loose objects are gone from the contract entirely (2.1); the fork resolves `(pack_id, offset, len)` and `pack_pins` blocks the parent's dead-mark inside the sweep's own span (lines 93-94, 196-197; platform-facts #4). Structural, not a check.
2. "`layer` is the mutable string `objects/<owner>/<repo>` ... Layer must be the parent DO id": **resolved**. `borrowed.owner_repo` is the parent's `meta.repo_id` (defaulted from `out.repo_id`, line 139); keys are `r/<repo_id>/packs/<pack>` (2.2, 8.4), so rename and delete-and-recreate touch `meta` only.
3. "Pins cover ref tips at page 0 only; objects imported on later pages ... are unpinned and sweepable": **resolved**. `begin` pins the entire live set in one span and `page` serves only rows whose pack is pinned to this fork (lines 106-108); a post-begin parent push lands in an unpinned pack the join cannot return, and `fork_sync` re-pins idempotently.
Caveats from the first pass: "Duplicate `POST /fork` races past the `exists` check" -> the claim is the `ON CONFLICT` miss on `meta.parent` inside the target's own span, loser gets `Conflict` -> 409 (lines 55-56, A2): **resolved**. "Parent pin obligation is a promise made by another DO" / deleted parent -> the obligation is now the parent's own `WHERE` clause against its own tables while the DO lives; SQLite/R2 loss underneath is still assumed away and fails loud, not silent (Known limits, line 235): **partially resolved**. "Fork clone of a large repo pays per-object GETs" -> resolved in design via coalesced `read_entries` grouped by owner; in code it is hostage to deviation 1: **resolved by dependency**. "Cross-tenant read of another prefix is by design" -> unchanged, one bucket, section 12: **not resolved, stated as policy**. "Import of a 2M-object parent is ~400 alarm ticks" -> now ~100 slices at 20,000 rows each, still O(N) per generation: **not resolved, by design**. "the fork advertises no HEAD" -> `meta.head` copied through `begin` and written (lines 99, 145); `unborn`/`symref-target` emission is `wire` (1.1 rule 6): **resolved** (modulo deviation 5's `peeled`). "`readObject` for a sha in the index returns `null` mid-`streamPack`" -> pins make the case unreachable, and a genuinely missing byte is `Storage` -> band-3 `ERR` (section 10), an explicit failure: **resolved**.

## Crash walk-through
Kill inside `fork_claim`'s span: atomic — either no `parent` row or a complete claim plus a queued `Fork` job. Kill after the span but before `jobs::rearm` resolves: the job row sits `queued` with no alarm until the next scheduled firing (the Janitor's 15-min tick) or the next enqueue+rearm from any route; a retried `POST /fork` gets 409 and cannot re-kick it — delayed, not lost. Kill awaiting `/_do/fork/begin` or `/page`: the parent's span committed (`forks` row + `pack_pins` for the fork's `repo_id`) or rolled back; the fork side is untouched, `Err` retries with the cursor kept (4.4), and a retried `begin` re-pins idempotently — the pin set can only grow. Kill in the post-`begin` insert loop or mid-page insert loop: one sync span, rolls back, `fork_state` stays `pinning`/`importing`, the page is re-fetched; `INSERT OR IGNORE` + the `EXISTS` guard make replay a no-op. Kill after the last page writes `done` but before the dispatcher applies `Done`: the next firing re-enters the `_` arm, whose drain is idempotent. Kill mid-drain: the parent's `DELETE FROM pack_pins` span either ran (the resent unpin deletes 0 rows) or did not; the fork deletes its `fork_unpins` rows only after a 200 (line 172). Two inherited wedges apply to `JobKind::Fork` exactly as to every kind: an isolate kill mid-slice leaves the row `running`, which `WHERE state='queued'` never re-selects (4.2), and a `dead` job has no re-enqueue path unless A4's boot rule ("any per-repo maintenance kind") is read to cover `Fork` — the proof does not say.

## Concurrency walk-through
Two `POST /fork` on one fresh repo: serialized spans; the loser's `meta.parent` insert reports `changes()=0` -> `Conflict` -> 409, exactly one claim. Parent push vs `fork_parent_begin`: the push's new pack is either not yet `live` at pin time (never pinned, never returned by `page`'s join) or live and pinned — both statements run inside the parent DO, so there is no third interleaving; the new objects stay invisible to the fork until `sync`. Parent `GcSweep` vs `begin`: the dead-mark's `AND id NOT IN pack_pins` is evaluated inside the sweep's own span — sweep-first means the pack is `dead` and never listed; pin-first means the sweep skips it. Child `unpin` vs parent sweep: the unpin only ever names packs the fork's own sweep already dropped (`objects` rows gone first), so any order is safe. `fork_sync` during `pinning`/`importing` -> `Conflict` (line 63); push and fetch during import -> `Conflict` from the `push_begin`/`fetch_v2_entry` first-line gate (line 206) — the input gate never opens because the check precedes every await. Two forks of one parent are scoped by `pack_pins.fork_repo` throughout. The un-analysed race is detach-during-import (deviation 6).

## Interop check
The wire never changes: a finished fork advertises its copied refs and `HEAD` (a symbolic `meta` row, so `wire` emits `symref-target:`/`unborn` correctly per 1.1 rule 6), and `fetch` streams verbatim normalized entries (2.1), so the bytes are indistinguishable from a non-fork repo — once the reads reach the bytes. As written they do not: `send_set`'s first commit-region read of a borrowed pack resolves `r/<fork_repo>/packs/<parent_pack>`, a key never written (the proof admits as much at line 202), so the added scenario's clone fails with `Storage` -> pre-stream 500 or one band-3 `ERR` mid-stream. During import the door is half open: `/_do/refs` and `/_do/ls-refs` are ungated, so the advertisement succeeds and only then does `fetch` hit `Conflict` — which section 10's pre-response table does not map for the upload-pack path (A2 assigns 409 only to receive-pack before its header), so the client sees an unspecified 5xx. `peeled:` lines are lost end-to-end because the sibling's `list_refs` never reads the A5 column (deviation 5). Push to a fork works as designed: the advertised copied refs make the client send only new objects, and thin-pack bases resolve through the owner-tagged `push/lookup` response (line 205). `POST /fork` is curl-only — stock git has no fork verb — stated at line 239.

## Blockers
- The owner redirect covers two of five byte-read paths. `send_set` (9.3/9.5), `GcMark` (5.1's section-9 loop) and `GcConsolidate` (5.2) must resolve each pack through `owner_of`/`for_repo`; until the REGISTRY says so, a fork cannot serve a fetch, run a GC mark, or finish a detach — each dies with `Storage("missing r/<fork>/packs/...")`. The fix is uniform and the helper exists, but the added scenarios cannot pass without it.
- `fork_sync` does not sync refs: `INSERT OR IGNORE` keeps the fork's snapshot targets forever (deviation 2), so the most common upstream event — a moved branch — never propagates. One-statement fix, but it changes what the route does.

## Caveats
- 20,000 sequential per-row inserts per page with no `spent_80pct` check (deviation 3): a page that cannot fit the slice wall clock livelocks to a `dead` job. Halve `PAGE_ROWS` or resume mid-page — the `(sha, pack_id)` cursor already supports it.
- No re-enqueue path for a `dead` `Fork` job: a parent outage longer than the ~2 h backoff window (4.4) bricks the fork mid-import with the parent's pins still held. Either a resume route or an explicit statement that A4's boot re-enqueue covers `JobKind::Fork`.
- `fork_detach` is ungated mid-import (deviation 6); refuse unless `fork_state = 'done'`.
- `fork_unpins` rows blocked by a child's pin are retried only when some future `GcSweep` re-enqueues `Fork`; `/_do/fork/unpin` does not kick the child's drain, so quiet repos hold grandparent pins indefinitely — a leak, not a dangling read.
- `/_do/fork/begin`, `/page` and `/unpin` trust the caller-supplied `fork_repo`; nothing binds it to the caller's identity. Moot under the single-tenant auth of section 12; worth a line if that changes.
- Compile hygiene (deviations 4-5): `Serialize` on `PinPack`/`ObjRec`/`RefRow` (or map to `RefDto`-shaped strings), `serde` on `gix-hash`/`bstr` or hex conversions, `meta_opt`, `env()`, and the `push/lookup` response shape all need one-line write-backs to the siblings.
- Unverified per memo/contract: `Request::new_with_init` on the DO-to-DO path (shared with two-phase-push), `worker::Bucket: Clone` for `for_repo` (fallback `env.bucket("BUCKET")`), `SqlStorageValue: From<Option<String>>` (fallback two INSERT forms).
- Retention granularity — a whole pack pinned including dead weight, abandoned forks pinning a parent's live set forever — is stated plainly in Known limits (line 234); a real cost, not a defect.
- Header placeholders `__V2_VERDICT__`/`__F__` unfilled (line 3).

## Verdict
risky. A real redesign, not a patch: all three first-pass blockers are closed structurally — whole-pack pins inside the parent's own sync spans, immutable `repo_id` addressing, and a page query that cannot return unpinned rows — and the crash story walks clean. But the normative content is short two edits: the owner redirect reaches `write_pack` and ingest lookups only, so the added "fork lifecycle" scenario dies at `send_set`'s first borrowed-pack read and the "detach" scenario dies inside `GcConsolidate`; and `fork_sync` cannot move a ref. Both fixes are mechanical and the helper already exists — with them this is lands-with-caveats; as written, the feature does not run.
